Sharks, AI is consuming so much power that data centers are literally overheating, and 40% of their electricity bill goes toward cooling GPUs. Operators overcool their entire facilities just to prevent micro-hotspots, burning millions of dollars in wasted electricity.

One micro‑hotspot. A whole facility overcooled.

Data centers running high-density AI training develop localized micro-hotspots. To protect expensive GPUs, operators aggressively overcool the entire facility, wasting massive amounts of electricity.

Here's the problem up close. A few racks running heavy AI training get dangerously hot. To protect those GPUs, operators blast cooling across the whole hall, so every other rack sits at about 18°C while the cooling units run flat out.
